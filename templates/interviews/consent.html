1. What this interview is about

This interview asks about one recent or memorable sensory overload experience, what triggered it, how it affected you, what helped, and how a support concept like PurrStone might or might not fit that situation.

2. Study boundary

This interview is for design research only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

3. Your rights as a participant

  • You can skip any question.
  • You can stop the interview at any time.
  • Please do not share sensitive personal information that you are not comfortable having saved in the transcript.
  • Your responses will be saved as a transcript for researcher review.

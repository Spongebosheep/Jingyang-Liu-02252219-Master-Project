PurrStone Stakeholder Interview Project

Case: PurrStone

Research workspace: Stakeholder interviews

MVP scope: One participant record, one sensory overload interview protocol, and one complete interview-to-review loop. The wider pages show workflow structure, not a full stakeholder CRM.

Research aim

Collect stakeholder insights about sensory overload, everyday overwhelm, coping behaviours, support needs, public-use acceptability, and reactions to the PurrStone support concept through a bounded AI-led semi-structured interview.

Interview workflow

  1. Review participant record
  2. Select interview protocol
  3. Confirm consent and study boundary
  4. Complete AI-led semi-structured interview
  5. Save transcript and generate structured summary
  6. Researcher reviews output and evidence record

Study boundary

This interview is for design research only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Participants can skip questions or stop the interview at any time.

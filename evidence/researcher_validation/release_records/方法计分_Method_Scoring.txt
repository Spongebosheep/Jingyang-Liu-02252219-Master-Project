
方法与计分 / Method and Scoring

- Formal sample: P01 P01, P02 P02, P03 P03; n=3.
- Design: each researcher completed one Manual and one Prototype condition across three controlled synthetic protocols.
- Objective rows: 24 Topic judgments + 36 Evidence-item judgments = 60 rows.
- Authority: GoldReviewer01 completed six-item Gold v0.8.0; the v0.7.1 frozen archive is method/freeze provenance only.
- Correctness: final decision compared with Gold, including explicitly allowed alternative handling.
- False approval: an issue-bearing evidence item approved as acceptable.
- Source correction: retaining the wrong sole source for an Edit source item is incorrect and is counted as a false approval.
- PC11 timing: objectively timed active review minutes recorded for each condition, excluding instructions, breaks and the questionnaire; reported descriptively for n=3.
- Pilot-01 and Pilot-02 are procedural/release evidence and are excluded from formal n=3.
- No inferential statistics are claimed from this small exploratory sample.

## English

Formal 32 retained one attempt for each of 32 planned runs. MVP completed 16/16 and the prompt-only Baseline completed 10/16, yielding 10 complete pairs. Automatic records were more complete for MVP on Action Inspectability (102/102 vs 93/102), Action Consistency (86/86 vs 74/86), Missing-information Visibility (8/8 vs 7/8), and Topic Coverage Representation (80/80 vs 50/80); both conditions passed Participant-control Compliance (2/2).

In the blinded A/B review, mean scores were equal for Q1 protocol coverage (4.4 vs 4.4) and Q3 source fidelity (4.9 vs 4.9). Baseline scored higher on Q2 follow-up quality among only four applicable pairs (5.0 vs 4.5), while MVP scored higher on Q4 participant clarity/control (5.0 vs 4.1) and Q5 researcher reviewability (5.0 vs 4.5). Exact rating agreement was 84/88 and all 88 ratings were within one point. Six pairs had unanimous MVP preference, two unanimous ties, and two Baseline-versus-Tie disagreements; no pair had unanimous Baseline preference. Descriptive Cohen's kappa was 0.688 on the original blinded A/B/Tie labels; the MVP/Baseline/Tie recoding sensitivity value was 0.643, and the two label spaces must not be conflated.

The earlier MVP-only analysis assessed final-reviewed S7/S8 evidence: MP was 8/9 (4/5 unique), resolved UCR was 3/46 (3/27 unique-text sensitivity), initial agreement was 45/46, and Cohen's kappa was 0.789. The later Phase 1/2 analysis was conducted primarily to fill the Baseline evidence gap and assessed pre-review drafts from the 10 completed pairs: Baseline MP was 45/45 and UCR was 0/220; deduplicated results were 9/9 and 0/46. Raw agreement was 100% for both measures, but kappa was not estimable because both coders used only one category. Text-identical MVP counterparts permit equivalent mapping, but were not a separate independent MVP evaluation. The two stages must not be pooled or directly compared.

Formal and post-formal SLC/RAC must remain separate. Formal MVP SLC was 9/9; Formal Baseline SLC was 0/0, INCOMPLETE, and not evaluable. Frozen MVP RAC was 12/26, with 12/12 complete among executed actions. Frozen Baseline RAC was 0/10 and INCOMPLETE; because no Formal review actions completed, this is not interpretable as observed 0% review quality. A later deterministic Baseline S7/S8 diagnostic yielded SLC 9/9, 10/10 source checks across all candidates, RAC 12/12, and 2/2 passing runs. It does not change Formal Baseline completion of 10/16.

## Claim boundary

These results support bounded scripted workflow feasibility and descriptive comparison under one frozen protocol. They do not establish generally better interviews, target-researcher utility or adoption, scalability, production readiness, or superiority to commercial platforms.

Interview prompt 1 · Opening
Before we begin, this interview is about sensory overload. In this project, sensory overload means a moment when a place or situation feels overwhelming because there is too much noise, light, movement, crowding, smell, touch, or other sensory input. For example, this might happen in a supermarket, on public transport, or in a busy public space. I will ask you about one specific moment like this: what happened, what made it overwhelming, how you reacted, and what support might have helped. Later, I will also ask for your reaction to a sensory-support concept called PurrStone. This is a design research interview, not medical advice. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Is it okay to continue?
Participant response 1 · Opening
Yes, it is okay to continue.

Thank you. Let us continue with the next topic.
Participant response 2 · Experience
On a crowded train, the carriage stopped between stations while alarms sounded. The noise and crowding made me feel trapped and overwhelmed.
Interview prompt 3 · Triggers & signs

Participant response 3 · Triggers & signs

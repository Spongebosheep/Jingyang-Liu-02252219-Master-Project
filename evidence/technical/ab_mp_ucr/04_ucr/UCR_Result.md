The sole disagreement was SF-008-P02. Its final label is `unsupported`. The other unsupported rows are SF-008-P03 and SF-008-P06.

## Procedure and boundary

The administrator froze a pre-existing blind proposition proposal before labels were entered. The two coders then completed independent supported/unsupported coding. A separate coder-confirmation round for the common inventory was not performed; therefore the 46-row inventory is fixed but not coder-consensus segmentation.

The result is supplementary within-MVP evidence over nine reviewed items. No Baseline UCR exists because the corresponding S7/S8 Baseline runs did not complete. The 46-instance result is primary for this supplementary analysis; the 27-unique-text result shows duplication sensitivity.

The original returns and resolution workbook are not in this shareable folder because their OOXML metadata are identity-bearing. `Return_Hashes.csv` preserves exact hashes; `UCR_Audit.xlsx` is the clean reporting derivative.

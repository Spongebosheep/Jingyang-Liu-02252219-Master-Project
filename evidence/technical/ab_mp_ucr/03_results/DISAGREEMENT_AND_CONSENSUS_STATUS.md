# A/B disagreement and consensus status

The two independent returns contain four one-point Q2 score disagreements and two overall-preference disagreements. A blind consensus round was not performed. The original ratings remain the only human judgements, and every disagreement is retained in `AB_DISAGREEMENTS_UNRESOLVED.csv`. The package reports initial agreement and condition-mapped pair dispositions; it does not report a consensus score or a post-consensus 100% agreement rate.

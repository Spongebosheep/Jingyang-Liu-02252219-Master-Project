
# Unsupported-content rate (UCR) status

Status: **RESOLVED_SUPPLEMENTARY**.

UCR Phase 2 used two independent coders on a frozen 46-row proposition inventory. Initial exact agreement was 45/46 (97.8%) and Cohen's κ was 0.789. Coder01 marked 3/46 unsupported; Coder02 marked 2/46 unsupported. The only disagreement, SF-008-P02, was resolved as unsupported.

The final within-MVP UCR is **3/46 = 6.5%**. Because the inventory contains repeated proposition text, the unique-text sensitivity result is **3/27 = 11.1%**. Unsupported proposition IDs are SF-008-P02, SF-008-P03 and SF-008-P06.

This is not an MVP-versus-Baseline comparison. The inventory covers nine final-reviewed MVP evidence items and no corresponding Baseline item. The inventory was administrator-frozen from a pre-existing blind proposal without a separate coder-confirmation round, so the 46-row denominator must not be called coder-consensus segmentation.

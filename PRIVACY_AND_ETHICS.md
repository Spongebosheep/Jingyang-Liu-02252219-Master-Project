# Privacy and ethics boundary

This repository is public. Its evidence layer has therefore been separated from
the controlled research archive.

## Included publicly

- Synthetic technical scenarios, model-call records, validators and rendered
  technical Evidence Records.
- Coder-labelled A/B, meaning-preservation and UCR materials that do not contain
  researcher identities.
- Participant-coded aggregate researcher-validation results (`P01`–`P03`).
- A de-identified Gold workbook (`GoldReviewer01`).
- Public-safe figures and redacted screenshots.
- The final thesis report, which states the study scope and limitations.

## Retained offline and not published

- Names, identity sheets and organisation-identifying records.
- Signed consent forms and superseded consent copies.
- Raw participant return ZIPs, raw response/manual workbooks, and original
  researcher-validation Evidence Record HTML files.
- Pilot source packages and identity-bearing audit logs.
- Ethics applications, approval links/pointers and internal governance records.
- The working `db.sqlite3`, database backups, `.venv`, caches and local `.git`
  directory.
- Original consultation forms where public redistribution consent and
  de-identification could not be established from the package alone.

The thesis reports the ethics and consultation evidence at the level needed to
understand the method. The internal approval pointer is intentionally not
reproduced here. Excluding controlled files from GitHub does not mean they did
not exist; it preserves the distinction between examination evidence and
material suitable for unrestricted public distribution.

The public researcher-validation analysis code is provided for method
transparency, but a complete rerun requires the controlled raw inputs retained
offline. Public aggregate files must not be used to infer participant identity.
